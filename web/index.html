Layers

Drag on the canvas with Type (T) to move the selected text, or drag the box's edge handle to rewrap it. With no text selected, click to place text or drag out a paragraph box. Text stays editable when saved.

Transform

Drag to move, square corners to resize, or the round handle to rotate. Hold Shift to snap rotation to 15°.

Groups move with the Transform tool (V) and align as one unit. Blend Pass through lets adjustments inside reach the layers below the group.

Retouching samples the layers beneath it. Paint with Clone (S), Healing (J) or Dodge and burn (O) while this layer is selected.

Adjustment layer

Affects the original and layers below it. Move this layer to change its scope; paint its mask to target an area.

Mask

Paint to hide or restore part of this layer. The original pixels are preserved; the mask moves with the layer.

Layer styles

Topmost layers appear in front. Photo layers keep their original files. Save a project to preserve editable layers, masks, and effects.

Selection

Refine edge pulls the outline onto edges in the photo, for hair and soft subjects. The magic wand samples the original photo.

Arrange

    Guides are saved with the project. Drag them with the Transform tool (V); drag one off the image to remove it. Layers snap to guides, the grid, the image edges and center, and other layers.
